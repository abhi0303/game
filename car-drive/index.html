    Graphics

    High is the full picture. Medium and Low lower the resolution, shadow detail, view distance and rain or snow for less heat and longer battery. Add ?perf to the address to see the frame rate.

    On foot

    WASD walk · Shift run

    Space jump, or climb what is in front

    Click the game to aim with the mouse; click to punch or shoot

    Fists: look at someone and keep clicking for jab, cross, hook, uppercut (he steps in to reach); someone on the ground gets a kick; Shift breaks off and runs

    Running uses stamina (the yellow bar); falls from high up hurt (the green bar is health)

    0 fist · 1 gun · 2 bow

    Bow: hold the mouse to draw, let go to shoot (the longer the draw, the further it flies)

    F get into the vehicle, horse or bike beside you

    Esc free the mouse pointer

    Driving

    W accelerate · S brake, then reverse

    AD steer · Space handbrake

    Shift nitro · C view from the driver's seat

    R repair the vehicle and its tyres · F get out (stopped)
